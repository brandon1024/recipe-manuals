# lentil-tacos.7

# NAME

Lentil Tacos

# INGREDIENTS

- Lentils, Cooked (125mL, 1/2 cup)

- Red Onion (half, finely diced)

- Jalapeño (2, finely diced)

- Tomato (half, finely diced)

- Garlic (2 cloves, minced)

- Vegetable Broth (62.5mL, 1/4 cup)

- Olive Oil (30mL, 2 tablespoons)

- Sriracha (5mL, 1 teaspoon)

- Taco Seasoning (15mL, 1 tablespoon)

- Taco Shells

# INSTRUCTIONS

  
In a pan, combine **red onion** (half, finely diced), **jalapeño** (2,
finely diced), **tomato** (half, finely diced), **garlic** (2 cloves,
minced), **olive oil** (30ml, 2 tablespoons). Cook at medium heat for 10
minutes.

\[step\]  
Add **vegetable broth** (62.5ml, 1/4 cup), **taco seasoning** (15ml, 1
tablespoon), **lentils, cooked** (125ml, 1/2 cup) and cook until liquid
has evaporated, approximately 3 minutes.

\[step\]  
Serve, garnishing with toppings of choice.

# COMMENT

- Estimated Prep Time: 10m

- Estimated Cook Time: 30m

- Difficulty: Easy

# ADDITIONAL NOTES

Serving with a hard shell wrapped in a soft tortilla is pleasant and not
as messy.

Corn, cheese and salsa are great for garnishing. Pico de gallo or a home
made sweet salsa can take the recipe to another level.

Not all taco seasonings are created equal. It's a good idea to taste and
adjust salt levels if needed.
